PACE YOUR DAY
• Step goals that grow gently from your own recent baseline, with a caution line before you overdo it
• Rest days and flare days never drag down your averages or break a streak
• When your resting heart rate or HRV say your body needs recovery, today's step range shrinks on its own

LOG WHAT MATTERS, AS OFTEN AS YOU LIKE
• Mood and body check-ins through the day

CHALLENGES WITH FRIENDS (OPTIONAL)
Steps, sleep, activity, logging or weight challenges where each person scores against their own daily goal, so different bodies can take part fairly.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• App lock with a password, fingerprint or face unlock
• Export or delete all your data at any time
• An account is optional and only used for challenges

Available in English, Spanish and Traditional Chinese.

ZebraPace is a self-tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and does not replace your doctor, physical therapist or other care providers.

## App content → Health apps declaration (Health Connect permissions)

Review language is English. One justification per permission; all of them read only. ZebraPace never writes to Health Connect.

**General description of the app's health features**

```
ZebraPace is an energy-pacing and symptom tracker for people with Ehlers-Danlos syndrome, post-exertional malaise (PEM) and dysautonomia, where doing too much on a good day can cause a crash days later. Health Connect data is read only to pre-fill the user's own log and to adjust the user's suggested daily step range. It is stored on the device, never sold, never used for advertising, and never sent to third parties. The only exception is user-initiated: if the user joins an optional challenge with friends, that day's total (for example steps or sleep hours) and whether they met their goal are shared with that challenge group.
```

**READ_STEPS**

```
Pre-fills the user's daily step count. Steps drive the core pacing feature: a step goal that grows gently from the user's own recent baseline, with a caution line to help them avoid post-exertional crashes. Also used in step challenges the user chooses to join.
```

**READ_EXERCISE**

```
Shows the workouts recorded today so the user can review them and add them to their activity log (type and duration). Logged activities feed the user's exertion vs. delayed-symptoms insights.
```

**READ_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED**

```
Shows the active energy of each workout the user imports and is used to estimate the workout's METs (intensity), shown in the user's insights and in the PDF summary the user can give their doctor.
```

**READ_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED**

```
Health Connect returns a workout's total calories together with the exercise session, and reading workouts fails without this permission. ZebraPace uses it only as part of reading the workouts the user chooses to import.
```

**READ_DISTANCE**

```
Health Connect returns a workout's distance together with the exercise session, and reading workouts fails without this permission. ZebraPace uses it only as part of reading the workouts the user chooses to import.
```

**READ_HEART_RATE**

```
Shows the heart-rate range during each imported workout and during sleep. The user's insights compare how hard a day was (peak heart rate) with how their body felt in the following days, which helps people with PEM recognise their limits.
```

**READ_RESTING_HEART_RATE**

```
Compared only against the user's own history (about the last 21 days) to estimate recovery. On days resting heart rate is clearly above the user's usual, the suggested step range shrinks so they don't overexert, and the app shows the reason.
```

**READ_HEART_RATE_VARIABILITY**

```
Compared only against the user's own history (about the last 21 days) to estimate recovery. On days HRV is clearly below the user's usual, the suggested step range shrinks so they don't overexert, and the app shows the reason.
```

**READ_SLEEP**

```
Pre-fills last night's sleep duration and sleeping heart rate in the user's sleep log, which feeds their daily energy estimate. The user reviews the values before saving.
```
