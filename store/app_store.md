## Trust & Safety → App Privacy

Matches `ios/Runner/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`. Apple counts data as collected only when it leaves the phone and is kept longer than needed to answer the request. The health log itself stays on the iPhone, so it isn't collected.

- **Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Yes
- **Tracking:** No, for every type

| Data type | Linked to the user | Used for tracking | Purpose | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contact Info → Email Address | Yes | No | App Functionality | Optional account for challenges (one-time code sign-in) |
| Contact Info → Name | Yes | No | App Functionality | Display name shown to the challenge group |
| Health & Fitness → Health | Yes | No | App Functionality | Weight in weight challenges, and rest/flare days |
| Health & Fitness → Fitness | Yes | No | App Functionality | Daily challenge values: steps, sleep hours, active minutes |
| Diagnostics → Crash Data | No | No | App Functionality | Anonymous error reports (no user ID, email or health data), deleted after about 90 days |

Not collected:
- **Location:** the place typed for weather is sent to Open-Meteo only to answer that request and isn't kept, so under Apple's definition it isn't collected. (Play's Data safety form is stricter, which is why Play lists it.)
- **Apple Health data:** stays on the iPhone, except the challenge totals above.

If Apple asks about Health data use in review: HealthKit data is never used for advertising or sold, and never stored in iCloud.

## Trust & Safety → App Accessibility

Accessibility labels say what the app supports in its main tasks, so only tick what you've tried on the iPhone yourself:

- **Larger Text:** the app follows the iPhone's text size and has its own size setting on top. Tick it only after checking Vitals and Plan at the largest size with nothing cut off.
- **VoiceOver, Voice Control, Sufficient Contrast, Differentiate Without Color, Reduced Motion:** not tested. Leave them unticked for now.
- **Dark Interface:** No. The app has no dark mode.
- **Captions, Audio Descriptions:** don't apply (no video or audio).

Leaving the section empty is allowed for now; Apple shows "not provided".
